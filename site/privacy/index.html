1. Who we are

MJ Codeworks (“we”, “us”) is an independent app development studio based at 25A Palandiamman Koil Street, Pallikaranai, Chennai 600100, Tamil Nadu, India. This policy covers our website, mjcodeworks.org. Our apps are covered by their own policies, linked above.

2. Information we collect

This website does not ask you for any personal information. There is no sign-up, no contact form and no newsletter, and we do not set cookies.

3. Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and our domain uses Cloudflare for DNS and may be delivered through Cloudflare's network. As with every website, these providers receive your device's IP address, browser details (user agent), the page requested and the time of the request so that they can deliver the page, keep it secure and prevent abuse. They handle this information under their own privacy policies. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not combine it with any other data.

4. Cookies and local storage

We do not use cookies. If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it next time. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

5. Links to other services

Some links take you to other services, such as Google Maps for directions or Google Play for our apps. Those services handle your information under their own privacy policies.

6. If you contact us

When you email or call us, we receive your email address or phone number and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply, keep a reasonable record of the conversation and protect our services, and we never sell it.

7. Children

This is a general business website and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct or erase it by emailing hello@mjcodeworks.org. Because this website does not collect personal data, there is usually nothing to delete on our side. For requests about one of our apps, please see that app's policy.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
