How we work

From idea to Play Store, without the chaos.

Four clear steps with a real outcome at each one, so you always know what's happening and what comes next.

  1. STEP 01

    Discover

    We listen first: your users, your goals and what success looks like. Then we agree on scope, timeline and budget.

    Project brief & estimate
  2. STEP 02

    Design

    Wireframes first, then a clickable prototype you can try on your own phone before any production code is written.

    Clickable prototype
  3. STEP 03

    Build

    Short sprints with a fresh test build every week, so you see real progress and can steer early.

    Weekly test builds
  4. STEP 04

    Launch & grow

    We prepare the store listing and policies, ship to Google Play and keep improving the app after launch.

    Live on Google Play
